\scriptsize\textit{Notes:} This table reports the bilinear A/B/C/cross decomposition of the connectivity-driven approximation of untreated firms' CBAs toward their treated counterparts' CBAs, using the \emph{clause-share vectors} as the outcome. For each untreated firm $i$ and CBA period $t$, the outcomes are: $A = s_{u,t} \cdot s_{T,t}$ (both moving); $B = \bar s_{u,i} \cdot s_{T,t}$ (focal anchored at pre-treatment average); $C = s_{u,t} \cdot \bar s_{T,i}$ (partners anchored at pre-treatment average); $\text{cross} = (s_{u,t} - \bar s_{u,i}) \cdot (s_{T,t} - \bar s_{T,i})$ (interaction of focal and partner shifts around their pre-treatment anchors). Anchors are firm-specific averages over $t \in \{1, 2\}$; firm fixed effects absorb the firm-specific constant $\bar u_i \cdot \bar T_i$ (resp.\ $\bar s_{u,i} \cdot \bar s_{T,i}$), so by linearity of OLS the identity $\hat\beta_A = \hat\beta_B + \hat\beta_C + \hat\beta_{\text{cross}}$ holds exactly; the residual is reported per panel. The $B{+}C{+}\text{cross}$ column reports the algebraic sum of the three single-outcome coefficients (and the root-sum-of-squared SEs, $^\dagger$, which is an upper bound under perfect positive correlation; the true SE of the sum is the SE of the $A$ column). Connectivity weights are uncorrected per-worker bilateral flows (\texttt{bilateral\_conn\_pw}), identical to Exercises A/B/C. Connectivity is scaled to 1 at the 90th percentile of the spillover sample at year 2009. Pre regression is the placebo on the within-pre-window contrast ($\text{cba\_period} \in \{1,2\}$); for the $\text{cross}$ outcome this regression is mechanically degenerate because $(u_t - \bar u_i)(T_t - \bar T_i)$ is constant within firm across the two pre-periods (the symmetric anchor implies $u_1 - \bar u_i = -(u_2 - \bar u_i)$), so we report $\beta_{\text{cross}}^{\text{pre}} \equiv 0$. Sample restriction: untreated firms in the lagos balanced spillover panel. Panel A includes establishment, industry $\times$ period, mode-month $\times$ period, microregion $\times$ period FE; Panel B adds union $\times$ period. Standard errors clustered at the establishment level. *\,$p<0.10$, **\,$p<0.05$, ***\,$p<0.01$.
