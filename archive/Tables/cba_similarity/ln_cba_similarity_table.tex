\scriptsize\textit{Notes:} This table reports spillover-effect estimates of log CBA content similarity on connectivity. The outcome is the natural log of each similarity measure; observations with similarity equal to zero are excluded. For each untreated firm $i$ and CBA period $t$, similarity is computed against a \emph{flow-weighted average} of the clause vectors of treated firms with pre-treatment worker flows to $i$; the weights are proportional to the bilateral worker-flow volume.
