    \textit{Notes:} This table summarizes the robustness of the current-connectivity log wage results across alternative specifications. Panel~A reports direct effects, comparing directly treated establishments to untreated establishments with zero pre-reform connectivity; Panel~B reports spillover effects on the full sample of untreated establishments. Column~(1) is the baseline specification with quartile-bin controls. Columns~(2)--(3) replace quartile bins with $N$-bin controls. Column~(4) adds workforce-characteristic controls. Columns~(5)--(8) add controls for local treatment exposure within each establishment's industry $\times$ microregion cell, measured as the share of directly treated establishments (columns 5 and 7) or the share of employment in directly treated establishments (columns 6 and 8). Columns~(5)--(6) enter these controls linearly and columns~(7)--(8) as quartile bins cut on the 2009 value; both are interacted with year fixed effects, so the columns differ only in functional form. Local exposure is normalized to its 90th percentile among untreated (spillover-sample) establishments in both panels, so the direct and spillover coefficients in a column share one scale. The spillover/direct ratio divides the Panel~B estimate by the Panel~A estimate from the same column. Standard errors clustered at the establishment level in parentheses. *** p$<$0.01, ** p$<$0.05, * p$<$0.10.
