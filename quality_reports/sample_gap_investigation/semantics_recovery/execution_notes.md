# Execution corrections and provenance limits

- An early launcher used the wrong working directory; no production output was written. The existing `launcher_wrong_cwd.log` preserves that failure.
- The first pooled-dictionary script specified `append, keep(ambiguous_workers)` for early-year count files that did not contain that variable. Stata stopped before dictionary creation. The corrected script appends the files, fills absent ambiguity counts with zero, and then aggregates. `build_wide_dictionary_append_error.log` preserves the failed form; `build_wide_dictionary.log` is the successful run with all employment assertions.
- The first uncertainty-support audit attempted `collapse (count)` on string `contract_id`, which Stata rejects. It was changed to the sum of a nonmissing-contract indicator. The failed log is `uncertain_mode_eligibility_count_error.log`; the successful audit finds no CBA-root matches for any of the ten uncertain establishments.
- A first attempt at the full historical-CBA input comparison failed with `file /tmp/St00002.000001 already exists`, `r(602)`, before writing its historical temporary copy. Separate sandboxed Stata jobs can share the same PID-based temporary name. The comparison was rerun with `STATATMP=/tmp/rais_cba_recovery_historical_compare`. Subsequent jobs use distinct temporary directories.
- To avoid assuming that this collision was harmless to earlier work, the full CBA construction was repeated with `STATATMP=/tmp/rais_cba_recovery_verified_cba`, writing to `CBA_verified`. `compare_cba_builds.log` establishes exact equality of all membership-relevant CBA records between the two builds. `verify_full_merge.do` checks the final full population against the independently built CBA records and all upstream RAIS establishment-year keys and employment/geographic values. Its completion marker is required by the final manifest.

The worker-count extraction does not silently claim to reproduce the random choice among geographically different tied spells. It records all such spells, uses a representative only for counting, and computes conservative bounds on whether any choice can alter the pooled mode. Ten full-population assignments remain potentially sensitive; none of their roots appears in the active firm-CBA input. Their exact municipalities are irrelevant to the recovered membership, and are not claimed to be observed historical assignments.

The historical source directly records the 2007–2016 worker pool and the later change to 2009–2016. Its dictionary-writing block is commented, and no October 6 execution log or original dictionary was found. Thus the evidence can recover and validate the geographic rule and establishment membership without identifying every byte of the original runtime state. A dated October 6 dictionary/run log or the actual script version that wrote it would settle that narrower execution-provenance question.
