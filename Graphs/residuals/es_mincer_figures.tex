    \begin{tablenotes}
        \small
        \item This figure shows dynamic DiD coefficients for the
        Mincer-residualized log December wage. Panel~(a) compares treated firms
        against zero-connectivity controls (Panel~A sample); Panel~(b) shows
        spillover coefficients from interacting annual dummies with
        pre-treatment connectivity to treated firms, scaled to the 90th
        percentile. The wage outcome is residualized on \texttt{cell\_id},
        \texttt{cell\_id}$\times$age polynomial controls. Base year is 2011;
        dashed vertical line marks the onset of treatment. 95\% confidence
        intervals based on standard errors clustered at the firm level.
    \end{tablenotes}
\end{threeparttable}
