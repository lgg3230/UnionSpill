\textit{Notes:} This table reports spillover effects of the 2012 ultractivity reform on the number of clauses in collective bargaining agreements at untreated firms. Column~1 reports the total number of clauses. Columns~2--4 decompose that total into wage clauses, employment clauses, and other clauses. The sample is restricted to untreated establishments in the Lagos balanced-panel spillover sample. Connectivity is the firm's pre-reform worker-flow exposure to treated firms, normalized by the 90th percentile of the 2009 spillover-sample distribution. ``Post $\times$ Connectivity'' reports the average post-reform spillover effect, and ``Pre $\times$ Connectivity'' reports the corresponding placebo coefficient estimated from pre-reform CBA periods only. All specifications include establishment fixed effects, CBA-period interactions with industry, negotiation month, and microregion, and quartile-bin controls for the pre-treatment outcome, pre-treatment log employment, and average per-worker worker flows during 2007--2011. Standard errors clustered by establishment are reported in parentheses. The pre-trend p-value is from a test of differential pre-trends with respect to connectivity across pre-reform CBA periods. *** p$<$0.01, ** p$<$0.05, * p$<$0.10.
