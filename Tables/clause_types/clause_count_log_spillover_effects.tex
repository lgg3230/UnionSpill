\textit{Notes:} This table reports spillover effects of the 2012 ultractivity reform on the (log) number of clauses in collective bargaining agreements at untreated firms. The sample is restricted to untreated establishments in the Lagos balanced-panel spillover sample. Connectivity is the firm's pre-reform worker-flow exposure to treated firms, normalized by the 90th percentile of the 2009 spillover-sample distribution. ``Post $\times$ Connectivity'' reports the average post-reform spillover effect, and ``Pre $\times$ Connectivity'' reports the corresponding placebo coefficient estimated from pre-reform CBA periods only.
