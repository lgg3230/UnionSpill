All specifications include establishment fixed effects, CBA-period interactions with industry, negotiation month, and microregion, and quartile-bin controls for the pre-treatment outcome, pre-treatment log employment, and average per-worker worker flows during 2007--2011. Standard errors clustered by establishment are reported in parentheses.
