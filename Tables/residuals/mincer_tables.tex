\item \textit{Notes:} This table compares direct and spillover effects of Brazil's 2012 ultractivity reform on raw and Mincer-residualized log December wages (\textit{lr\_remdezr\_w} and \textit{lr\_remdezr\_resid}). Standard errors clustered at the establishment level in parentheses. *** p$<$0.01, ** p$<$0.05, * p$<$0.10.
