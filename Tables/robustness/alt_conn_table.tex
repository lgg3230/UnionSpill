\begin{tablenotes}
\item \textit{Notes:} This table assesses the robustness of the main spillover results to alternative connectivity measures. The sample is restricted to untreated establishments in the Lagos balanced panel. Panel~I uses \textit{avg\_ftreat\_pf\_n}: the average yearly share of worker flows directed to treated firms, computed across pre-treatment year pairs (2007--2011). Panel~II uses \textit{totaltreat\_pf\_n}: the proportion of total pre-treatment flows going to treated firms. Both measures are scaled to the 90th percentile of the spillover sample (untreated, balanced panel) in 2009, matching the normalization of the main specification. Post $\times$ Connectivity captures the average spillover effect for 2012--2016; Pre $\times$ Connectivity is a placebo test using 2009--2011. The \# CBA Clauses outcome uses CBA negotiation periods rather than calendar years. All regressions include establishment fixed effects, year fixed effects interacted with two-digit industry, microregion, and negotiation-month indicators, and quartile-bin controls for pre-treatment per-worker pairwise flows (2007--2011) and pre-treatment log employment, each interacted with year fixed effects. Pre-trend $p$-value is from the joint test of pre-treatment placebo coefficients. Standard errors clustered at the establishment level in parentheses. *** p$<$0.01, ** p$<$0.05, * p$<$0.10.
