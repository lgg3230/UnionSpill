\item \textit{Notes:} This table tests whether the spillover effect of Brazil's 2012 ultractivity reform on log wages is robust to controlling for union coverage of treated firms. The sample is restricted to untreated establishments in the Lagos balanced panel. Connectivity is per-worker pairwise flows to treated firms (2007--2011), normalized to the 90th percentile. Column~(1) is the baseline specification. Column~(2) adds union $\times$ year fixed effects to the absorb, absorbing all time-varying union-level heterogeneity. All specifications include establishment fixed effects, year fixed effects interacted with two-digit industry, microregion, and negotiation-month indicators, and quartile-bin controls for pre-treatment log wages, log employment, and per-worker pairwise flows (2007--2011), each interacted with year.
