This table reports two-sided randomization-inference p-values for the spillover coefficient under four schemes for reshuffling the treated label across firms. In every scheme the treated label is permuted within strata of predetermined destination-firm characteristics, holding the number of treated firms fixed at 12,276, and placebo connectivity is rebuilt as $C_i^{(r)}=\sum_j W_{ij} D_j^{(r)}$. Industry cells use the true two-digit CNAE division. The observed estimate is $\hat\beta=0.0051$ (log wages) and $\hat\beta=0.0066$ (log hourly wages), identical across schemes by construction. `Treated firms swappable' counts treated firms that share a stratum with at least one untreated firm (only these can change status); the remainder are frozen. Based on 1,000 placebo draws. $^{*}p<0.10$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{***}p<0.01$.
