\begin{tablenotes}
\item \textit{Notes:} This table assesses the robustness of the headline difference-in-differences estimates to extending the pre-treatment window back to 2007. Firm-year outcomes for 2007--2008 are reconstructed from the full RAIS following the same worker-selection and deflation procedure as the main sample, and reproduce the 2009 values to machine precision. The reform (Brazil's 2012 ultractivity ruling, effective September~25, 2012) extended existing CBAs indefinitely upon expiration. Each panel reports the pooled estimate on the published 2009--2016 window and on the extended 2007--2016 window, under two sample definitions: \textit{(a)} the headline balanced-2009--2016 firms with 2007--2008 observations added where the firm operated (pre-period unbalanced), and \textit{(b)} firms operating in every year 2007--2016 (balanced throughout). Comparing the 2009--2016 rows across \textit{(a)} and \textit{(b)} isolates the role of firms entering after 2007; comparing the 2009--2016 and 2007--2016 rows within a sample isolates the added pre-period. ``Post~$\times$~Treatment/Connectivity'' is the pooled post-reform estimate ($t\geq2012$ versus the pre-period); ``Pre~$\times$~Treatment/Connectivity'' is the corresponding placebo, which replaces the post indicator with one for the pre-treatment years (2009--2010 in the 2009--2016 window, 2007--2010 in the 2007--2016 window) relative to the 2011 reference. The pre-trend $F$ $p$-value is a joint test that the dynamic event-study coefficients for 2007--2010 are zero (2011 is the omitted base). All regressions include establishment fixed effects, year fixed effects interacted with two-digit industry, microregion, and negotiation-month indicators, and quartile-bin controls for pre-treatment per-worker pairwise worker flows and establishment size interacted with year. The direct panel uses Panel~A (zero-connectivity controls); the spillover panel uses untreated firms with connectivity normalized to the 90th percentile of the spillover sample in 2009. Standard errors clustered at the establishment level in parentheses. *** $p<.01$, ** $p<.05$, * $p<.10$.
