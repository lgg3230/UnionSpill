Panel~D reports spillover effects, where exposure is the firm's pre-reform worker-flow connectivity to treated firms, normalized by the 90th percentile of the 2009 spillover-sample distribution. ``Post'' is the average post-reform effect and ``Pre'' the corresponding placebo from pre-reform periods only. All specifications include establishment fixed effects, period interactions with industry, negotiation month, and microregion, and quartile-bin controls for the pre-treatment outcome, pre-treatment log employment, and average per-worker worker flows during 2007--2011. The log clause count uses the same controls as the clause count, except that $\ln(\text{clauses})$ drops the few firm-period observations with exactly zero clauses.
