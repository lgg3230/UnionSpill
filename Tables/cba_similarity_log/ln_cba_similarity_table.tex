\scriptsize\textit{Notes:} This table reports spillover-effect estimates of log CBA content similarity on connectivity. The outcome is the natural log of each similarity measure; the outcome is $\ln(1+\text{similarity})$, so zero-similarity observations are retained. The coefficient is interpretable as the percentage change in similarity associated with a one-unit increase in connectivity. For each untreated firm $i$ and CBA period $t$, similarity is computed against a \emph{flow-weighted average} of the clause vectors of treated firms with pre-treatment worker flows to $i$; the weights are proportional to the bilateral worker-flow volume. Only untreated firms with at least one treated counterpart in the bilateral flow data enter the sample. Connectivity is scaled to 1 at the 90th percentile of the spillover sample. Panel A includes establishment, industry $\times$ period, mode-month $\times$ period, and microregion $\times$ period fixed effects, with pre-treatment bins of the outcome, log employment, and total flows absorbed.
