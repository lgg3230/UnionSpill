\begin{tablenotes}
\item \textit{Notes:} This table presents difference-in-differences estimates of the effects of Brazil's 2012 ultractivity reform on CBA content. The CBA Value Score is the wage-equivalent weighted sum of subgroup clause counts, using the subgroup-level weights estimated in Lagos (2026). Clause Count is the total number of clauses in the CBA (unweighted). Columns (1)--(3) report direct effects on treated establishments relative to control groups of increasing breadth. Column (4) reports the spillover effect for untreated establishments: the interaction of post-reform indicator with pre-reform connectivity (share of workforce overlapping with treated firms, normalized to the 90th percentile). All regressions include establishment fixed effects and CBA-period fixed effects interacted with two-digit industry, microregion, and negotiation-month indicators, and quartile-bin controls for pre-treatment per-worker pairwise worker flows (2007--2011) and pre-treatment establishment size, interacted with CBA-period fixed effects. Standard errors clustered at the establishment level in parentheses. *** p$<$0.01, ** p$<$0.05, * p$<$0.10.
