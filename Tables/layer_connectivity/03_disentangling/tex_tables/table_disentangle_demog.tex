\textit{Notes:} This table tests which worker group's connectivity to treated firms drives the spillover effect. Column groups correspond to a specific connectivity variable (the regressor), identified by checkmarks at the bottom. Layer-level regressions restrict the sample to workers in the indicated layer (cross-layer or own-layer as shown) and absorb firm FE and year FE. Firm-level regressions collapse to firm$\times$year and absorb firm FE and year FE. In all cases the regressor is the focal connectivity variable interacted with a post-2012 indicator (S\'{u}mula 277 reform). All regressions are restricted to untreated, balanced-panel firms. Connectivity is scaled to the 90th percentile of the control sample at 2009. Standard errors clustered at the firm level. $^{***}p<0.01$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{*}p<0.10$.
