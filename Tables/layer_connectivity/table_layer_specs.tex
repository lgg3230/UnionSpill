\textit{Notes:} All regressions restricted to untreated, balanced-panel firms in the Lagos sample. Connectivity is scaled to the 90th percentile of the control sample at 2009 and is interacted with a post-2012 indicator (S\'{u}mula 277 reform). The pre-trend (placebo) coefficient uses a fictitious treatment onset at 2010, estimated on the pre-reform period (2007--2011) only. The pre-trend F-test $p$-value tests the joint significance of all pre-trend event-study coefficients; small values indicate detectable pre-trends.

\textit{Outcome levels.} Columns (1) and (2) use \textit{layer-level} outcomes: each observation is a firm--layer--year cell, and the connectivity measure captures the share of that layer's outflows reaching treated firms. Column (3) uses \textit{firm-level} outcomes and the aggregate firm-level connectivity, restricted to the subset of firms present in the layer sample.

\textit{Fixed effects.} Within-firm specification (1) absorbs firm$\times$year FE; identification comes from cross-layer variation within the same firm--year. Cross-firm specification (2) includes microregion$\times$year, industry$\times$year, and mode$\times$year FE, controlling for local labor-market trends, sector shocks, and contract-type composition. Firm-level specification (3) includes firm FE together with a standard set of time-varying controls (log employment, wage quartile, and sector trends).

\textit{Layer definitions.} Panel A groups workers into three education bins: no high-school diploma (no HS), completed high school (HS), and tertiary or above (higher). Panel B collapses HS and higher into a single group (2-bin partition).

Standard errors clustered at the firm level. $^{***}p<0.01$, $^{**}p<0.05$, $^{*}p<0.10$.
